UX Case Study

Podspace — Podcast Rental Studio App

The Opportunity

With the rapid growth of content creators and remote podcasters, finding professional, high-end, soundproof studios with premium recording equipment has become extremely chaotic and complex.

Most existing solutions are static, lack real-time room availability, require long-drawn email threads, and do not offer customized high-end mic/equipment selection packages.

Key Goals

  • Seamless Scheduling: Direct visual calendar selection for soundproof podcast studios.
  • Equipment Customization: Easy hardware bundles addition (e.g. Shure SM7B, Rødecaster Pro II).
  • Interactive Space Booking: Real-time pricing models calculated by hours/slots.

UX Core Focuses

01
Discover Rooms

Dynamic sorting by capacity, acoustics, and pricing.

02
Custom Hardware

Dynamic addon selectors with real-time bill calculations.

03
Instant Pass

NFC/QR-based studio check-in for digital convenience.

Empathetic User Research

Leveraging HRM research models, I conducted survey cycles and built user personas for freelance audio producers and corporate corporate marketing teams.

"As an independent creator, I don't just want a room—I want to know exactly what microphonic setup I have access to, and know that it's booked for my slot instantly."

— Aarav S., Indie Podcaster

User Insights

  • Acoustic Assurance: Users need proof of soundproof rating (e.g., NC-20 level isolation) and room size.
  • Setup Friction: Setting up audio interfaces waste 15-20 minutes of recording bookings. Solution: Pre-configured professional setups available as toggle options in the app.

Target Persona Profile

Riya Sharma
Freelance Audio Producer

Goal: Launch a weekly tech interview series with no audio engineering overhead.

Pain Point: Hidden rental costs, complicated scheduling, and sub-par room isolation.

Sleek Aesthetic Design System

Podspace uses a dark mode concept designed for nighttime creators, focusing on reducing fatigue, conveying professional acoustics vibes, and displaying neon indicators.
