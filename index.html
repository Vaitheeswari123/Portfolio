Problem Statement

Content creators often struggle to find, compare & book podcast studios easily.

Goal

To design a platform that helps users discover studios, reserve slots & make payments seamlessly.

Key Goals

  • Discover Studios: A centralized platform where users can find and compare podcast studios with ease.
  • Reserve Slots: Real-time availability calendar for quick and hassle-free slot reservations.
  • Seamless Payments: Integrated payment flow enabling users to complete bookings in one smooth experience.

UX Core Focuses

01
Discover Studios

Find & compare podcast studios with filters for location, pricing, and amenities.

02
Reserve Slots

Real-time calendar-based slot booking with instant confirmation.

03
Make Payments

Secure, seamless in-app payment flow for a frictionless checkout.

User Research Findings

To identify the core challenges in the audio/podcast recording studio ecosystem, we conducted user research and interviews with various content creators. Here are the key findings and insights:

80%

Difficulty Finding Suitable Studios

80% of participants reported spending significant time searching across multiple platforms to compare studios.

INSIGHT

Users need a centralized platform with filters and detailed studio information.

75%

Lack of Pricing Transparency

75% of participants expressed frustration with hidden costs and unclear pricing structures.

INSIGHT

Displaying clear pricing upfront increases trust and confidence.

70%

Complex Booking Process

70% of participants found existing booking systems confusing and time-consuming.

INSIGHT

A streamlined booking flow can reduce user frustration and improve conversion rates.

85%

Importance of Studio Details

85% of participants wanted detailed information about: Equipment availability, Studio photos, Amenities, and Location.

INSIGHT

Providing comprehensive studio information helps users make informed decisions.

65%

Need for Flexible Scheduling

65% of participants preferred real-time availability and flexible booking slots.

INSIGHT

Users value the ability to view and reserve available time slots instantly.

Quantitative Analysis

To understand user expectations and market opportunities for PodSpace, a review analysis was conducted on leading studio rentals and podcast platforms.

Review Ratings Comparison

Giggster (Direct Competitor) 4.8/5
Peerspace (Direct Competitor) 4.8/5
Soundtrap (Indirect Competitor) 4.0/5
Zencastr (Indirect Competitor) 3.5/5

Key Insights

  • Direct studio-booking platforms receive higher satisfaction scores (4.8/5).
  • Users value simple booking processes and transparent pricing.
  • Podcast creation tools score lower due to technical complexity and collaboration challenges.
  • There is an opportunity for PodSpace to combine easy studio discovery with creator-focused features.

Design Opportunities for PodSpace

  • Streamline studio search and booking flow.
  • Provide real-time studio availability.
  • Offer transparent pricing and secure payments.
  • Enable creators to manage bookings from a single dashboard.
  • Reduce friction during studio reservation and confirmation.

Impact on Product Design

The findings influenced the design of PodSpace by prioritizing:

Fast studio discovery Simple reservation process Clear payment flow Booking status tracking Creator-friendly user experience
